WATER FORMULA — OBSERVATIONS
============================
Working notes for the greenhouse water model. Append to this; do not rewrite
earlier entries, since a later reading is only worth anything next to the ones
before it.

Verbatim source of session 1 is in water-formula-prompt.txt.


WHAT THE WIKI SAYS
------------------
  After each growth stage, a crop loses between 2-3 Water Level, which can be
  mitigated by the +50% Water Retain and +100% Improved Water Retain effects,
  and amplified by the -30% Water Drain negative effect respectively.

  If a crop has negative Water Level during a Growth Stage, it has a chance not
  to advance to the next stage.


UNITS AND UNKNOWNS
------------------
  water_level    integer, observed range -100 .. +100
  loss_per_tick  2..3 unbuffed, per the wiki. Whether the range is random per
                 tick or fixed per crop is NOT yet known.
  retain         +50% or +100%, from an adjacent crop
  drain          -30%, from an adjacent crop
  P              the full growth tick PERIOD. NOT yet measured. Every "next
                 tick in N" below is time REMAINING, not the period.
  death          assumed at -100. NOT yet confirmed.
  "Lasts for"    a string in the water bucket lore. Derived and rounded by the
                 server, so it is weaker evidence than a water_level reading.

WHAT THESE TWO SETS DISAGREE ABOUT
----------------------------------
  +50% retain multiplied the wheat's remaining time by 1.47 and the rose's by
  exactly 1.00. A single multiplier on duration cannot produce both.

  The likeliest explanation is that the loss is an INTEGER per tick and retain
  divides it before rounding. A loss of 3 reduced by 50% is 2 after rounding, a
  visible change; a loss of 2 reduced by 50% is 1 after rounding one way and 2
  after rounding the other. That would make +50% sometimes do nothing at all,
  which is exactly what B2 shows, and it fits the wiki's 2-3 range being a
  range rather than a number.

  If that holds, "lasts for" is a rounded presentation of an integer tick count
  and is a poor thing to fit a formula to.


WHAT TO MEASURE NEXT
--------------------
  1. P, the growth tick period. Everything above is in minutes and cannot be
     turned into per-tick figures without it. Two consecutive "next tick"
     countdowns, or the gap between two ticks landing, gives it.
  2. water_level immediately BEFORE and AFTER a tick, for the same plant, at
     each of none / +50% / +100% / -30%. The difference IS the loss per tick.
     This is worth more than any number of "lasts for" strings.
  3. Whether the loss varies tick to tick for one unchanged plant. Two or three
     consecutive ticks on the same plant answers it.
  4. Whether death is at -100 exactly, and what happens at it.
  5. Whether two retaining neighbours stack, and whether they cap.
  6. Whether Effect Spread carries retain further than one crop.
  7. Whether a plant in water debt actually skipped a stage, and how often. The
     wiki says "a chance", so this needs several ticks to say anything.
