# MagicAddons

MagicAddons is a Fabric mod for Hypixel SkyBlock that adds quality of life features and enhancements across various areas of the game.
Made for Minecraft 26.1.2 and 26.2 currently, with plans to update to newer versions as Hypixel does too.

## Farming

- **Greenhouse Planner**: build a greenhouse from a saved preset with ghost crops showing what goes where, marks for what is wrong, missing or in the way, and a chat line that gives you easy access to the crops and soils needed by the preset.
- **Greenhouse Presets**: up to three plots per preset, an in-game editor with a plant palette, target markings for the mutations you are growing, and import and export in several formats.
- **Plant Tracking**: recognises every recorded crop and mutation stage in your greenhouses and keeps stage, age, water and hunger across sessions, with a diagnostic tool hook for correction when needed.
- **Water Model**: predicts each plant's water tick by tick, including retain and drain effects (Soggybud support included!), and how long a plant has until it fully grows or dies.
- **Greenhouse Prediction**: lets you visually see what your greenhouse will look like (with some limitations) up to 10 ticks ahead of time.
- **Highlights**: water and harvest highlights in the world.
- **Reminders**: ready to harvest, needs water, decay, snoozling asleep, noctilume time switch, chorus collision, with reminders before and at the growth tick (with support for multiple profiles!).

## Foraging

- **Safari Helper**: highlights safari mobs, tracks which are still uncaught in which zone and lists which player is assigned to that zone, optional messages to party chat, and warns with a title and sound when a sparkling mob is detected.

## Combat

- **Mob Highlight**: highlights mobs by name, a predefined preset or mob, or advanced rules for mobs not yet added explicitly.
- **Highlight Markers**: visual indicator for differentiating multiple mob types.
- **Custom Rend Sound**: detects when a player has rended and plays a configurable sound.

## Mining

- **Powder Coating Hider**: hides powder coating particles while wearing Divan armour.

## Misc

- **Smol People**: draws other players visually smaller.
- **Appearance**: customize the mod's UI to your own liking.

## More to come

MagicAddons is actively developed and new features are added regularly. Feature requests and bug reports greatly help development, so if something is missing or not working the way it should, please open an issue.
